## How to use this list

### Per-firm verification checklist (10 min each)

1. **LinkedIn company page** — confirm employee count (target 15–80 for $5–50M revenue band)
2. **Their website's "Projects" page** — recent projects in the $1M–$15M range = right size; $50M+ projects = they're bigger than ICP
3. **State licensing search** — confirm GC license active (free, public)
4. **Google "[Firm name] revenue"** — sometimes returns Crunchbase / ZoomInfo / D&B estimates; treat as rough signal not gospel
5. **Find the right contact** — Principal first, Controller second. LinkedIn search: `"company-name"` + title.

### Outreach sequencing

- Run **Tier 1** through outreach templates (LinkedIn DM A or Email A from `gc-outreach-templates.md`)
- **Pace yourself** — 5 sends per week, not 20 in one day. Construction is a small world; bursty outreach gets noticed and looks desperate.
- **One follow-up after 5–7 business days**, then move on.

### Realistic conversion expectations

Per the math in `gc-outreach-templates.md`:
- 30 sends → ~5 responses → ~3 calls booked → ~2 calls held → ~1 design-partner conversation
- 17 firms in Tier 1 ≈ enough to land 1–2 design-partner conversations

If after 17 sends you've had zero responses, the issue is the message, not the targets. Check in with me before sending more.
