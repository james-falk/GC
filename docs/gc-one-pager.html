Product Summary

One system for the monthly pay-app cycle, change orders, and reconciliation — everything in sync.

Schedule of values, subcontracts, change orders, and pay applications stay in sync across subcontractors, internal teams, architects, and owners. No more reconciling Excel against Excel against email.

01
Problems addressed
  • Schedule of values, change order tracker, and forecast each maintained in separate Excel files that must reconcile manually.
  • Subcontracts, COIs, signed change orders, and pay-app PDFs scattered across drive folders, email, and paper files.
  • Subcontractor pay applications submitted in inconsistent PDF formats requiring manual re-entry.
  • Change orders approved by owners but not reflected in subcontract ceilings or SoV, causing invoices to be held.
  • Compliance documents (lien waivers, COIs, certified payroll, prevailing wage) missed at month-end.
  • Reconciliation required across Excel, AIA / Cantina-style billing tools, and QuickBooks.
02
At launch
Available day one
  • Single source of truth for schedule of values, subcontracts, change orders, pay applications, supporting documents, and audit trail.
  • Owner-approved change orders keep everything in sync — the subcontract ceiling, the schedule of values, and every affected line update at the same time. No manual re-entry.
  • Continuous drift detection surfacing anything out of sync on a dashboard before it stalls a payment cycle.
  • Subcontractor submission portal with line-item validation against contract ceiling.
  • AIA G702/G703-compatible pay-app generation, sworn statements, and magic-link approvals for architects and owners.
  • QuickBooks-compatible CSV export for AP and AR entries.
03
On the roadmap
Following six months
  • State-specific lien waiver generation — partial and final, conditional and unconditional, ready for the lender or owner.
  • Compliance document tracking — COIs, W-9s, certified payroll, and prevailing wage docs with expiration alerts; missing docs hold pay-app submission.
  • AI extraction from sub-submitted PDFs for subs who don’t use the portal — line items pulled into the same form for review.
  • QuickBooks two-way API integration — invoices and bills push automatically; payment status pulls back.
  • Quarterly WIP report generation — percentage-of-completion math handled automatically; matches what bonding companies and lenders expect.
  • Cash flow, project margin, and forecast dashboards for principal-level visibility across all active projects.
  • DocuSign / e-signature integration for in-app signing on contracts, change orders, and pay apps.
  • Mobile field interface for project managers and superintendents to enter percent-complete from the jobsite.
